## Our review

M7 is an infrastructure node, not the handheld implied by a generic off-grid-device roundup. Wired Ethernet and PoE are its defining advantage: one managed cable can provide connectivity and power at a fixed location. We would shortlist it for an indoor installation where network operations and maintenance matter more than carried battery operation. Judge complete system fit before comparing enclosure size or an advertised range figure.

## Choose it when

You have a fixed indoor location with suitable Ethernet/PoE infrastructure.

## Skip it when

The requirement is a battery handheld, outdoor solar node or standalone keyboard terminal.

## Compatibility and limitations

The source does not describe an integrated outdoor solar system or handheld battery package. Ethernet bandwidth is not LoRa message throughput. Its indoor role and regional RF model must be distinguished from the outdoor M6 and portable M1/M5.

## Alternatives

Compare the other individually documented ThinkNode models, Heltec Mesh Node T1 and Seeed Wio Tracker L1 Pro by deployment role.

## First evaluation

Use the correct model target and owned peer nodes; log delivery counts, delays, configuration recovery and actual power conditions on a repeatable fixture.

Keep the firmware version, fixture and failed/skipped/unobserved cases with the result. Documented ratings are not measurements performed by this project.

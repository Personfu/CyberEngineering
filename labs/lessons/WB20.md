# WB20 · Read protocol evidence offline

**Optional tool · Network forensics · Applied**

Trace a decoded field back to stored bytes and capture context.

## Materials

TShark/Wireshark and a harmless saved capture you own.

## Procedure

1. Record capture provenance.
2. Identify the packet bytes for one decoded field.
3. Compare a display filter with the original packet population.

## Expected result

A source/field/byte explanation and explicit observation limits.

## Failure cases and interpretation

A filter cannot recover traffic missing from the capture; encrypted payloads remain unavailable without authorized keys.

## Record

Keep the input/source version, command, relevant output and interpretation together. Label a fixture synthetic. Physical-device work needs the actual model, firmware and conditions. Do not report an unrun exercise as passed.

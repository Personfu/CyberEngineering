# WB14 · Budget an SDR receive path

**Physical device · RF engineering · Applied**

Record sample/gain settings and a safe input-level budget before interpreting data.

## Materials

Owned SDR and a known controlled reference input.

## Procedure

1. Identify half/full duplex and conversion resolution.
2. Calculate the input path and attenuation from the exact manual.
3. Record a short receive-only baseline and inspect clipping.

## Expected result

Keep raw samples, settings, input assumptions and negative cases.

## Failure cases and interpretation

An uncalibrated spectrum display is not a calibrated power measurement or a device identity.

## Record

Keep the input/source version, command, relevant output and interpretation together. Label a fixture synthetic. Physical-device work needs the actual model, firmware and conditions. Do not report an unrun exercise as passed.

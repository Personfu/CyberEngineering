# WB13 · Build an owned RFID fixture

**Physical device · RFID and evidence · Applied**

Separate model capability, implemented mode and repeated observation.

## Materials

Owned reference reader/tags and a supported PM3/Chameleon setup.

## Procedure

1. Choose reader versus emulator role.
2. Record client/firmware and mode support.
3. Repeat a bounded identification/response fixture and retain negative cases.

## Expected result

A versioned compatibility record and reference-fixture observations, not a general access-control conclusion.

## Failure cases and interpretation

Proxmark5 maturity and Chameleon Lite reader limitations are documented separately.

## Record

Keep the input/source version, command, relevant output and interpretation together. Label a fixture synthetic. Physical-device work needs the actual model, firmware and conditions. Do not report an unrun exercise as passed.

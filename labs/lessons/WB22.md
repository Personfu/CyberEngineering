# WB22 · Review hardware/software provenance

**Offline fixture · Hardware trust · Foundation**

Distinguish physical revision, firmware artifact and source-document identity.

## Materials

One reviewed product page and its primary-source receipt.

## Procedure

1. Read a source-linked feature.
2. Record document URL/retrieval/digest and whether it is manufacturer or maintainer evidence.
3. Distinguish a stated rating, roadmap item and measured result.

## Expected result

An inspectable feature/source/version table.

## Failure cases and interpretation

A downloaded document being hashed does not authenticate every claim inside it.

## Record

Keep the input/source version, command, relevant output and interpretation together. Label a fixture synthetic. Physical-device work needs the actual model, firmware and conditions. Do not report an unrun exercise as passed.

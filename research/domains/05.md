# KEPLER · Network Observability

**Evidence:** Proposed domain research framework. The equations below are author-defined candidate models, not measured findings or requirements quoted from standards.

**Research focus:** Partial observation under time uncertainty, benign drift and rare-event prevalence.

## Governing model

```text
z_t = (x_t-median(x_train))/(1.4826 MAD(x_train)); alert = I[z_t>tau]
```

x is an aggregate count or scalar; freeze training window and threshold before testing. MAD needs an explicit zero-scale fallback. Correlated samples invalidate naive per-row confidence intervals.

**Competing models:** Fixed global threshold; rolling mean/std; single-sensor detector.

**Required record schema:** Event time, ingest time, sensor pseudonym, aggregation interval, event count, missingness mask, maintenance label. Extend the schema with provenance digest, units, coverage window, collection permission, missingness reason and transformation revision. Use explicit null values for missing observations; do not turn missing into zero.

## Evaluation contract

Measure Precision-recall, false alerts per sensor-hour, event detection delay, missingness-conditioned recall. Freeze a mission-specific primary endpoint and acceptable resource cost before tuning. Compare the candidate and baseline on the same scenario/partition. Include a no-intervention or null fixture to detect accidental leakage. Report uncertainty at the independent unit—scenario, channel, device or team—and analyze the worst-performing subgroup rather than relying on a portfolio average.

**Adverse cases:** Drop sensor blocks; change benign mean; burst legitimate demand; skew clocks; lower anomaly prevalence.

**Graduate challenges:** Nonrandom missingness; time skew; base-rate collapse; legitimate operating change. For each challenge, distinguish a limitation of the estimator from a limitation of available evidence. Ask whether the parameter is identifiable, whether the mechanism transfers to a new environment and which observation could distinguish rival explanations.

**Resource plan:** Offline LANL aggregates or owned OpenTelemetry traces; no live scanning or packet injection. Before implementation, produce a concrete bill of materials, input size, memory/storage estimate, runtime bound and personnel-hours estimate. No universal cost or readiness claim is made here.

## Applicable data and its limits

[LANL Comprehensive Multi-Source Cyber-Security Events](https://csr.lanl.gov/data/cyber1/). Authentication/process/DNS/flow correlation and missing-observation studies. Split chronologically and by entity; the small red-team label set is incomplete; absence of a label is not a negative truth. Failed-auth inclusion is conditional on at least one successful auth. Resource budget: 58 days, approximately 12 GB compressed; plan streaming aggregation rather than full RAM loading. Current status: landing page and schema inspected. Access/rights: CC0 waiver stated by publisher; human-subject and public-release approvals documented.

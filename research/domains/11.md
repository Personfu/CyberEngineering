# LUNAR GATEWAY · RF, space, and remote systems

**Evidence:** Proposed domain research framework. The equations below are author-defined candidate models, not measured findings or requirements quoted from standards.

**Research focus:** Time-varying contact, trustworthy state and finite onboard resources.

## Governing model

```text
A_(t+1)=0 if verified contact else A_t+dt; delivery iff contact AND evidence_valid
```

A is age of information in seconds. Reset only on verified reception of a newer state. Include clock uncertainty, key expiry, storage limits and intermittent power in extensions.

**Competing models:** Always-connected policy; transmit-at-every-step scheduler; unverified receipt.

**Required record schema:** Relative mission time, contact flag, queue bytes, evidence age, power mode, verified digest, rollback state. Extend the schema with provenance digest, units, coverage window, collection permission, missingness reason and transformation revision. Use explicit null values for missing observations; do not turn missing into zero.

## Evaluation contract

Measure Verified delivery fraction, age p95, energy proxy, recovery latency, evidence storage. Freeze a mission-specific primary endpoint and acceptable resource cost before tuning. Compare the candidate and baseline on the same scenario/partition. Include a no-intervention or null fixture to detect accidental leakage. Report uncertainty at the independent unit—scenario, channel, device or team—and analyze the worst-performing subgroup rather than relying on a portfolio average.

**Adverse cases:** Missed contact; delayed receipt; key expiry in blackout; storage full; reset during staging.

**Graduate challenges:** Availability vs freshness; cosmic faults vs software faults; limited energy; no trusted wall clock. For each challenge, distinguish a limitation of the estimator from a limitation of available evidence. Ask whether the parameter is identifiable, whether the mechanism transfers to a new environment and which observation could distinguish rival explanations.

**Resource plan:** NASA cFS source as architecture study; synthetic contacts, emulated payload; passive RF resources only. Before implementation, produce a concrete bill of materials, input size, memory/storage estimate, runtime bound and personnel-hours estimate. No universal cost or readiness claim is made here.

## Applicable data and its limits

[NASA Core Flight System](https://github.com/nasa/cFS). Offline remote-operation state models and assurance traceability. Do not infer flight readiness or certification from source availability; study command handling and scheduling in an emulator. Resource budget: Pinned framework checkout and toolchain; submodules separately pinned. Current status: NASA-maintained repository inspected. Access/rights: Check each component license and export/distribution notes.

# ORION · PCB, Hardware & Firmware Trust

**Evidence:** Proposed domain research framework. The equations below are author-defined candidate models, not measured findings or requirements quoted from standards.

**Research focus:** Recoverable device state across interruptions and physical environmental changes.

## Governing model

```text
P_safe = sum_s P(s) I[invariant holds after recovery(s)]
```

s indexes a documented fault schedule. Probabilities are assumed until measured; enumerate cut points rather than claiming a physical radiation upset distribution.

**Competing models:** Single-slot update; checksums without versioned state; no cross-sensor redundancy.

**Required record schema:** Update stage, verified version, active slot, power condition, temperature, sensor residual, recovery state. Extend the schema with provenance digest, units, coverage window, collection permission, missingness reason and transformation revision. Use explicit null values for missing observations; do not turn missing into zero.

## Evaluation contract

Measure Recoverability fraction, boot latency, flash wear, energy and independent trusted-image availability. Freeze a mission-specific primary endpoint and acceptable resource cost before tuning. Compare the candidate and baseline on the same scenario/partition. Include a no-intervention or null fixture to detect accidental leakage. Report uncertainty at the independent unit—scenario, channel, device or team—and analyze the worst-performing subgroup rather than relying on a portfolio average.

**Adverse cases:** Reset at every update boundary; loss of metadata write; benign sensor drift; recovery image unavailable.

**Graduate challenges:** Atomicity across reset; boot dependency; drift vs fault discrimination; measurement probe loading. For each challenge, distinguish a limitation of the estimator from a limitation of available evidence. Ask whether the parameter is identifiable, whether the mechanism transfers to a new environment and which observation could distinguish rival explanations.

**Resource plan:** Owned development board or emulator, current-limited supply, logic analyzer; no invasive extraction or bypass methods. Before implementation, produce a concrete bill of materials, input size, memory/storage estimate, runtime bound and personnel-hours estimate. No universal cost or readiness claim is made here.

## Applicable data and its limits

[OpenTitan device life-cycle specification](https://opentitan.org/book/doc/security/specs/device_life_cycle/). Firmware recovery and device-state trust review. Project-specific architecture; does not validate a derivative device or certify physical fault resistance. Resource budget: Emulator first; optional owned low-voltage dev board, qualified hardware review. Current status: primary device-lifecycle documentation inspected. Access/rights: Documentation/source terms require review before copying implementation.

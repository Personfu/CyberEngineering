# 173 · MERCURY Handoff — Team Memory Retention

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Human factors and security learning.

## Thesis

Structured uncertainty handoffs reduce unsupported assumptions at shift changes.

## Model and mechanism

Use crossed task/participant design; outcome combines severe decision errors, omitted uncertainty and completion time. Cluster by team for intervals.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/12.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Consented tabletop crossover with task-balanced order and blinded grading; compare free-form notes with source-linked uncertainty handoff.

Candidate resource: HELIOS deterministic local fixtures. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Learning carryover; assessor bias; accessibility mismatch; team interdependence.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if severe errors rise or time savings vanish after adjusting for task difficulty.

## Delivery and resources

Consented tabletop study, blinded rubric, task-balanced crossover; no deceptive campaigns. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

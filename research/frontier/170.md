# 170 · CASSINI Safe Harbor — Recovery Reachability

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Cyberphysical and Operational Resilience.

## Thesis

A recovery plan with physical safe-set reachability can reject digitally valid but unsafe restoration order.

## Model and mechanism

Safe set S constrains state trajectory; plan repair actions only if a bounded transition model keeps x_t in S throughout. Uncertain bounds require conservative feasibility.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/10.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Offline process simulation and approved tabletop decisions; compare service-priority and constrained recovery on identical demand.

Candidate resource: HELIOS deterministic local fixtures. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Delayed actuator response; unknown state; incompatible safe sets; infeasible recovery.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if any chosen plan violates S within stated model bounds, or unknown states are silently assumed safe.

## Delivery and resources

Offline physical simulator; domain-qualified safety review before any bench actuation. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

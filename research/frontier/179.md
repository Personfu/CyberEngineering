# 179 · JAMES WEBB Lens — Defense Effect Identifiability

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Measurement, governance, and futures.

## Thesis

A preregistered causal diagram can expose when before/after security benefit cannot be identified.

## Model and mechanism

Y(1),Y(0) define potential outcomes; draw common causes of intervention and outcome. Estimate only with defensible overlap and adjustment, otherwise report bounded sensitivity.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/15.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Simulate intervention assignment tied to prior incident rate; compare naive change, stratification and known randomized assignment.

Candidate resource: HELIOS deterministic local fixtures. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Regression to mean; simultaneous control rollout; measurement drift; interference.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if the estimator recovers a benefit under null randomized outcomes or ignores absent overlap.

## Delivery and resources

Preregistered longitudinal design, offline counterfactual simulations, independent measurement reviewer. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

# 166 · CHANDRA Drift — Ephemeral Resource Evidence

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Cloud, Workload and Recovery Systems.

## Thesis

Lineage windows improve configuration-drift attribution for short-lived resources.

## Model and mechanism

Join desired revision, creation event and observed state by stable resource identity; reason about delayed observations with validity intervals rather than last-value overwrite.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/08.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Synthetic deployment histories plus optional owned OpenTelemetry traces; compare periodic snapshots with lineage-aware reconciliation.

Candidate resource: OpenTelemetry documentation. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Reused identifiers; missed deletion; delayed collectors; eventually consistent controllers.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if reported violations cannot be tied to a reviewed valid-time state or false alarms exceed a snapshot baseline.

## Delivery and resources

Google Borg traces for workload timing only; synthetic cloud control-plane and owned isolated restores. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

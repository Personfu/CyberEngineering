# 175 · PHOENIX Relay — Robust Recovery Scheduling

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Incident response and resilience.

## Thesis

A robust schedule reduces worst-case mission loss when repair durations are uncertain.

## Model and mechanism

Choose feasible order minimizing CVaR_alpha(L) over declared duration scenarios; compare mean-loss and tail-loss choices under identical prerequisites.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/13.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Supplied six-service schedule fixture plus stochastic duration extensions; hold out extreme duration scenarios and unavailable staff.

Candidate resource: HELIOS deterministic local fixtures. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Correlated repairs; hidden prerequisites; incompatible staffing; inaccurate tail model.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if tail reduction disappears outside the optimization sample or safety prerequisite is violated.

## Delivery and resources

Offline schedules, sanitized past incidents only if authorized; no automatic production response. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

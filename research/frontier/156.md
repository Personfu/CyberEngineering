# 156 · Psyche Witness — Sensor Agreement Under Drift

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** PCB, Hardware & Firmware Trust.

## Thesis

Cross-sensor agreement with explicit calibration uncertainty reduces false fault labels relative to fixed residual limits.

## Model and mechanism

Fit latent state x with y_i=H_i x+b_i+epsilon_i; separate slowly varying bias b_i from abrupt disagreement. Identifiability requires redundancy or an independent reference.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/03.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Replay synthetic thermal drift and missing channels; then use consenting owned-board measurements with traceable reference calibration.

Candidate resource: OpenTitan device life-cycle specification. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Shared calibration error; common-mode supply change; probe loading; reference drift.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if the method accepts a common-mode discrepancy or rejects benign drift more often at matched detection sensitivity.

## Delivery and resources

Owned development board or emulator, current-limited supply, logic analyzer; no invasive extraction or bypass methods. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

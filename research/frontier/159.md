# 159 · KEPLER Nightwatch — Rare-Event Calibration

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Network Observability.

## Thesis

A detector selected at realistic prevalence respects analyst alert capacity better than one selected by accuracy.

## Model and mechanism

PPV = sensitivity*pi/[sensitivity*pi + FPR*(1-pi)]. Estimate pi separately; evaluate event recall and false alerts per sensor-hour across temporal blocks.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/05.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Run the supplied offline detector benchmark, then evaluate public LANL aggregates after access and schema review. Freeze thresholds on validation data; test prevalence reductions.

Candidate resource: LANL Comprehensive Multi-Source Cyber-Security Events. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Concept drift; missing telemetry; clustered events; delayed labels.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if the detector exceeds the preregistered alert budget or gains vanish after time-block splitting.

## Delivery and resources

Offline LANL aggregates or owned OpenTelemetry traces; no live scanning or packet injection. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

# 178 · WISE Chronicle — Temporal Catalog Selection

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Authorized exposure and intelligence governance.

## Thesis

Catalog-addition timing should be modeled separately from exploitation onset in longitudinal evaluations.

## Model and mechanism

Treat addition date as observation process time; model interval-censored onset only when independent onset bounds exist. Avoid fitting exploitation hazards from addition dates alone.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/14.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Use the pinned KEV date/vendor aggregates for descriptive analysis; a causal extension requires separate incident and disclosure evidence that is not supplied here.

Candidate resource: CISA KEV issuer-maintained data mirror. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Delayed reporting; changing inclusion policy; duplicate or withdrawn records; survival bias.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute any interpretation that turns observed addition counts into population exploitation rates without a sampling model.

## Delivery and resources

Public CISA metadata plus synthetic owned inventory; no discovery of third-party assets. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

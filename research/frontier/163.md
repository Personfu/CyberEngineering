# 163 · ARTEMIS Keyring — Revocation Under Blackout

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Identity, Consent and Authority.

## Thesis

Explicit offline validity bounds reduce stale authority while quantifying denied legitimate work.

## Model and mechanism

Lease lifetime tau trades stale-grant window against task availability during disconnect; optimize declared costs under bounded time error.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/07.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Synthetic task/identity graph with intermittent contact; compare short leases, periodic review and event-based revocation.

Candidate resource: HELIOS deterministic local fixtures. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Clock uncertainty; emergency authority; delegated grant; unusually long disconnection.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if a policy exceeds its declared stale-authority bound or denial cost dominates every comparable baseline.

## Delivery and resources

Synthetic authorization graph, integer solver optional, identity owner and privacy reviewer. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

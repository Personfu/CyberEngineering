# 154 · LUCY Atlas — Proof Assumption Debt

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Formal Software Assurance.

## Thesis

Tracking dependencies between assumptions and proofs reduces misleading assurance after configuration changes.

## Model and mechanism

Use bipartite proof/assumption graph; invalidated assumption removes confidence from every dependent claim. Compute minimal assumption cut sets but never equate graph reachability with theorem validity.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/02.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Version a small protocol specification; vary queue bounds, scheduling fairness and clock monotonicity. Compare manual proof review with explicit invalidation reports.

Candidate resource: TLA+ specification examples. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Hidden axioms; proof tool version changes; wrong environment abstraction.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if reviewers fail to detect invalidated critical claims or generated warnings overwhelm the original workflow.

## Delivery and resources

TLA+ or an equivalent model checker, bounded reference interpreter, developer and formal-methods reviewer. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

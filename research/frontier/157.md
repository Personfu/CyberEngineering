# 157 · VOYAGER Passport — Evidence Decay in Dependencies

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Supply Chain & Provenance.

## Thesis

Expired provenance evidence changes remediation decisions independently of component age.

## Model and mechanism

Model artifact identity, build evidence and policy validity as separate graph attributes; rank unverified edges without assigning invented vulnerability probabilities.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/04.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Generate versioned synthetic SBOMs and provenance statements; hold out ecosystem names and ambiguous version ranges. Compare flat inventory checks with identity-bound validation.

Candidate resource: SLSA specification v1.2. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Version aliases; absent dependency edges; legitimate unsigned artifacts; transparency service loss.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if freshness metadata only redistributes warnings without improving adjudicated decision accuracy.

## Delivery and resources

SPDX/CycloneDX parser, provenance verifier and owned builds; public metadata snapshots. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

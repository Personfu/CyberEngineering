# 172 · JUNO Partition — Radiation and Trust-State Coupling

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** RF, space, and remote systems.

## Thesis

Separating physical fault recovery from authority recovery exposes unsafe combinations of individually valid states.

## Model and mechanism

Product automaton combines device health, verified image, authority lease and link state; invariants constrain valid operational combinations.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/11.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Enumerate synthetic resets and contact gaps with an owned software emulator; compare independent health and authority controllers.

Candidate resource: NASA Core Flight System. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Recovered image with expired authority; corrupted evidence index; repeated reset; key unavailable.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if any reachable state permits a mission action without required verified state; no radiation-rate claim is made.

## Delivery and resources

NASA cFS source as architecture study; synthetic contacts, emulated payload; passive RF resources only. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

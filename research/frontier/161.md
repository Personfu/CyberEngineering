# 161 · OSIRIS-REx Prism — Acquisition Coverage Bounds

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Forensic Science and Evidence Integrity.

## Thesis

Coverage-aware negative-evidence statements are more accurate than treating absent artifacts as nonoccurrence.

## Model and mechanism

Define observation opportunity O and event E; missing record does not identify P(E) without P(record|E,O). Report bounds when capture probability is uncertain.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/06.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Create synthetic cases with known ground truth and independent missing blocks. Ask blinded reviewers to assess conclusions with and without coverage maps.

Candidate resource: NIST Computer Forensic Reference Data Sets. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Event-dependent missingness; overwritten buffer; parser failure; acquisition changing state.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if confidence remains high when the relevant observation opportunity is absent.

## Delivery and resources

NIST CFReDS fixture selection; synthetic evidence first; read-only analysis with pinned tools. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

# 152 · ARTEMIS Eclipse — Evidence-Age Mission Control

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** Mission Systems & Architecture.

## Thesis

Scheduling evidence refresh by expected decision loss yields better assurance within a fixed observation budget.

## Model and mechanism

Each claim has age a, refresh cost c and loss l(a); select refresh actions to minimize sum l(a) subject to budget. The age-to-loss relation is an elicited assumption until evaluated.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/01.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Simulate claim changes, stale evidence and imperfect refresh tools; compare periodic and loss-prioritized refresh at identical analyst-hour budgets.

Candidate resource: HELIOS deterministic local fixtures. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Refresh failure; correlated claim changes; misleading freshness; tool unavailable.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if targeted refresh raises false acceptance or cannot outperform fixed refresh across plausible age-to-loss curves.

## Delivery and resources

Discrete-event model, graph inventory, one workstation, systems engineer and independent mission owner. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.

# 168 · WEBB Horizon — Channel-Held-Out Anomaly Study

**Evidence class:** Proposed. No operational benefit or complete implementation is claimed. **Domain:** AI Systems and Bounded Automation.

## Thesis

Channel-level holdout reveals transfer failures hidden by random timestep splits.

## Model and mechanism

Fit a predictor on train channels; calibrate threshold on validation channels; evaluate event precision, delay and per-channel confidence without point-adjustment shortcuts.

Record variables, units and validity limits in a typed schema. Start from the [domain specification](../domains/09.md), while keeping this mission's distinct mechanism and endpoint. Separate observable variables from latent assumptions; explain which parameters can actually be estimated from the data. Models are original proposals rather than formulas attributed to the references.

## Experiment and applicable data

Use Telemanom public SMAP/MSL telemetry after license and preprocessing review; compare persistence, robust residual and learned predictor.

Candidate resource: Telemanom SMAP/MSL anomaly data and original study. Follow its exact [acquisition and rights contract](../RESOURCES.md). Data not acquired in this extension cannot be described as a measured result. Establish at least one simple baseline and equalize its information/tuning budget. Use independent time, entity, artifact, team or scenario partitions; preregister the primary endpoint before final evaluation.

## Challenges and rival explanations

Operating mode confounding; label interval ambiguity; overlap from windowing; dataset age.

A successful graduate study must test a rival explanation and a null control, quantify parameter sensitivity and document a failure outside the original environment. Report operating cost, missing observations and uncertainty at the independent unit. Distinguish a small-model correctness result from full-system assurance.

## Falsifier

Refute if improvement disappears on untouched channels or whole-event credit masks most missed timestamps.

## Delivery and resources

Offline curated cases and public telemetry; model output is analysis advice with bounded authority. Deliver a reference state/schema, a pinned lawful fixture, baseline code, held-out results, a resource ledger and an independent review record. Numerical performance floors and compute ceilings are proposed by the accountable mission owner at design review; no budget or readiness level is invented here.
